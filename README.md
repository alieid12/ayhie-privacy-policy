# Ayhie — Privacy Policy

A single, general privacy-policy website for Ayhie's mobile applications and related services. No individual apps are named.

## Published URL (once GitHub Pages is enabled)

https://alieid12.github.io/ayhie-privacy-policy/

## Deployment

1. Open **Settings → Pages** in this repository.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select **main** and **/(root)**, then save.
4. Verify the public HTTPS URL before entering it in app-store metadata.

## Important accuracy and compliance checks

- **Required before publishing to app stores:** Replace the contact placeholder in `index.html` with a working privacy contact email or form. The store developer name must also match the entity identified here (Ayhie), or you must add the actual developer/entity name. Google Play requires this match.
- The general policy discusses **possible** data practices; for each Service, verify every actual SDK, permission, category of collection, processing purpose, recipient, retention and deletion procedure. Add service-specific supplements and prominent disclosures as needed.
- Keep **App Store Connect privacy nutrition labels** and **Google Play Data safety declarations** accurate, including SDK collection and cross-app tracking where applicable.
- If a Service offers accounts, provide an accessible account-deletion path and comply with applicable in-app and web deletion requirements.
- If children can use a Service, assess age and family rules separately.
- Confirm any legally required transfer safeguards, lawful bases, consent, opt-outs, deletion and consumer rights relevant to your audience.
- Update the effective date and policy whenever material practices change.
