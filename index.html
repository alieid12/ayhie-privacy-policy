01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Ayhie ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in mobile applications and related services that link to this policy (collectively, the "Services"). Features and data practices may differ among Services. Only the categories and processing activities relevant to the Service you use apply. We will provide additional in-app notices or supplemental disclosures where needed.

02

Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, information may include: (a) content and preferences you enter, such as settings, progress, tasks or files; (b) contact or account details you voluntarily submit through support or sign-in features; (c) device, app and technical information, including operating system, device identifiers, IP address, crash and diagnostic events; (d) usage and interaction events; (e) advertising identifiers and ad performance information; (f) purchases, subscriptions and transaction status provided by app-store or payment partners; and (g) information you explicitly choose to grant access to, such as photos, media, camera, microphone, notifications or approximate/precise location. This list describes possible categories, not a statement that every Service collects them.

03

On-device and transmitted data

Certain Services store settings, content, history or progress locally on your device. Data stored only on your device may remain there unless you remove it, reset the Service, or your device's operating system removes it. Other Services or optional features may transmit information to us or third-party providers to deliver cloud functions, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, purchases or support. A device-only feature does not necessarily mean its advertising or analytics SDKs operate offline.

04

Purposes and legal bases

Where applicable, we process information to operate requested features, save preferences, troubleshoot failures, secure the Services, answer inquiries, administer purchases, measure performance, display or measure ads, and meet legal obligations. Where laws require a lawful basis, we rely as appropriate on performing a contract, legitimate interests balanced against individual rights, legal obligations, or consent. Consent may be withdrawn where applicable.

05

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Some Services may integrate advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, app-store, payment or infrastructure providers. Such providers may receive technical data and identifiers under their own terms and privacy notices, depending on integration, settings and consent. Advertising may be contextual or personalized only where permitted and applicable requirements are met. We do not assume every Service contains ads or analytics. If a Service uses a provider, its SDK behavior and sharing must be reflected accurately in app-store disclosures and any required in-app notice. We do not sell personal information for money as a general business practice; certain advertising disclosures may nevertheless be treated as a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under some laws, if used.

06

Permissions and choices

A Service may request access to notifications, photos, storage, microphone, camera, location or other device capabilities only to provide relevant functionality. You can review or revoke most permissions in your device settings; doing so may disable related features. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have choices regarding tracking, personalized advertising and consent. Store and device privacy settings may also affect data processing.

07

Retention and deletion

Information kept only on your device is normally controlled through that device and may be removed by deleting the applicable data or uninstalling the Service, subject to backups and operating-system behavior. For information we or our providers hold, retention depends on its purpose, service needs and legal requirements; we aim to keep it only as long as necessary. You may request access or deletion through the privacy contact channel shown below, specifying the relevant Service without sending passwords or sensitive information. We will verify requests where necessary and explain any lawful exceptions. If a Service offers account creation, account deletion and associated data deletion instructions must also be available as required by the applicable platform; uninstalling alone may not delete a remote account.

08

Security and international transfers

We use or require reasonable organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of information processed, but no storage or transmission is completely secure. Providers may process information in other countries; where legally required, we apply appropriate safeguards to cross-border transfers.

09

Children and families

The Services are not uniformly designed for children. Age eligibility and age-specific disclosures depend on each Service and its store listing. Where a Service is directed to children, additional privacy protections, consent and advertising limitations apply. If you believe a child's information was collected improperly, contact us through the channel below.

10

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, portability of, or restriction or objection to processing of personal information, and you may have the right to withdraw consent or complain to a privacy authority. Rights are subject to applicable laws and verification. We will not discriminate for exercising legally protected rights.

11

Changes

We may update this policy when features, providers, legal requirements or practices change. The revision date at the top will be updated, and significant changes will be communicated through the Services or another appropriate channel when required.

12

Contact

Privacy requests and questions can be submitted using the privacy contact information displayed in the applicable Service's app-store listing or its in-app support section. **Before relying on this page for store submission, Ayhie must add a directly accessible privacy contact method here (such as an active email address or working contact form).**
